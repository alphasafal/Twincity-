Eco-Loop Building Agents 02 / Problem & solution

Close the loop. Keep it safe.

Problem

  • Buildings waste energy and drive peak demand
  • Uncontrolled LLM setpoints can be unsafe
  • Most dashboards observe — they do not act

Solution

Eco-Loop observes, reasons, validates, acts and measures outcomes inside an EnergyPlus digital building.

Eco-Loop Building Agents 06 / Feasibility & next

Feasibility, limits, future

Feasibility

  • Runs on local / cloud VM
  • Open-source local LLM (Ollama)
  • Reproducible scripts + clean-clone verified
  • Safe degraded mode when LLM is down

Limitations

  • EnergyPlus digital building only
  • No physical BMS connector yet
  • Savings are scenario-specific

Future

  • BACnet / Modbus · campus scale
  • Dynamic tariffs / carbon · physical pilot
