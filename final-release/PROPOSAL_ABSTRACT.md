# Proposal abstract

Eco-Loop Building Agents is a safe autonomous building-optimisation system operating against an EnergyPlus digital building. The platform reads zone temperature, occupancy, weather and energy data, evaluates energy-conservation strategies, validates every proposed action through a deterministic SafetyShield and writes approved cooling setpoints back to the EnergyPlus Clg-SetP-Sch actuator. A separate MCP server over stdio enables a locally hosted Ollama model to access building tools and produce structured contextual proposals, while a deterministic optimiser ensures reliable real-time numerical control and fallback operation. Under identical building, weather, occupancy and simulation conditions, the submitted comfort-first policy reduced simulated HVAC energy by 4.98%, total building energy by 1.31% and peak power by 1.47%, while maintaining zero occupied comfort-violation hours and zero comfort degree-hours. Eco-Loop demonstrates a practical, transparent and safety-controlled path from passive building dashboards to autonomous building operation.
